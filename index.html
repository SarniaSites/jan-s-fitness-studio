Before you join

The things people ask first. Anything else, call 07781 443663.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Membership is monthly, with no contract, no joining fee and no leaving fee.

When can I train?

Members have access 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Please keep the noise down in the car park when you leave late at night, as there are neighbours close by.

Can I try it before I join?

Yes. Trial sessions are free. Call, email or use the form below and we'll arrange a time to show you round and let you train.

I'm new to the gym. Is it for me?

Yes. The kit covers every level, from complete beginners to experienced bodybuilders. Every new member gets a free induction and a free workout programme.

Are there cheaper rates?

There are student rates for 16s and over, and senior citizen rates. Ask when you book your trial.
